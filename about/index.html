About Anirban De

I’m a founder, security leader, and investor based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

What drives my work

I founded grcOS to give security teams one place to run risk and compliance. My work at Replit and Uber has focused on turning enterprise security requirements into tools teams can use every day.

For more than 14 years, I've helped teams tackle difficult technology and security problems. I believe the best security makes people's work easier, not harder. My focus is building practical tools, clear processes and better ways for organizations to make decisions.

I’ve spent more than 14 years working with teams that need to balance speed, security and trust. I enjoy translating technical challenges into clear decisions and building systems that make the right thing easier to do.

How I approach problems

Start with the people affected. Understand what is getting in their way. Build something useful, measure whether it helps, and keep improving it.
