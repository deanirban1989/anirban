CASE STUDY / IDENTITY SECURITY & AUTOMATION

Making access reviews easier for people — and more reliable for security.

A current engineering effort to reduce manual effort when companies verify who can access important applications.

Organization: ReplitFocus: Identity governance, workflow automation and evidenceStatus: In progress

01 / THE PROBLEM

Manual reviews do not scale with fast-moving teams.

As organizations add applications and people change roles, checking access manually can become repetitive and difficult to track. Application owners need enough context to make a good decision without spending hours collecting information.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Design the review around the decision.

I am developing workflows that bring application access information to the people responsible for reviewing it, record their decisions and preserve supporting evidence for later verification.

  • Gather user and entitlement information from connected systems
  • Organize reviews around the application owner and relevant access
  • Make review decisions easier to complete and track
  • Record decisions and evidence for accountability and audit readiness

03 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

Connect data, decisions and evidence.

Collect

Read user and access data from source applications.

Review

Present relevant access to the accountable owner for a decision.

Record

Preserve decisions and evidence so follow-up is possible.

This describes the workflow at a high level rather than an internal production architecture.

04 / WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Less busywork. Better decisions.

The intended outcomes are lower reviewer effort, clearer access ownership and more consistent evidence. This work is in progress; I am not presenting unverified adoption, time savings or risk-reduction figures as achieved results.
