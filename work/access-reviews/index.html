01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

Access changes continuously. Reviews often don’t.

A fast-moving organization may use hundreds of applications with inconsistent identity models. A quarterly spreadsheet is not a reliable representation of who has access — or why.

01

Fragmented entitlement data

Okta, JumpCloud, Verkada, HackerOne, KnowBe4 and ZoomInfo expose different identity and entitlement models; some APIs do not provide complete organization-wide permissions.

02

Ambiguous identities

Emails change, service accounts have no employee record, and local application accounts may bypass the identity provider.

03

Reviewer fatigue

Owners receive lists without role meaning, privilege context or enough information to make defensible decisions.

04

Weak closure evidence

A “remove” decision is not proof that access was revoked. Remediation must be tracked and independently verified.

ENGINEERING OBJECTIVE

Convert inconsistent identity and entitlement data into complete, risk-informed, owner-accountable decisions with verifiable remediation and an auditable history.

Key design choice: preserve source identifiers and a point-in-time entitlement snapshot. Never silently merge ambiguous identities or treat a reviewer's approval as proof of technical removal.

05 / ENGINEERING TRADE-OFFS

Make the difficult edge cases first-class.

Incomplete provider APIs

Use adapter capability declarations, explicit coverage gaps and alternative exports when necessary. Do not label an incomplete inventory as fully reviewed.

Privileged and service accounts

Include privileged grants, local admins and non-human identities with designated accountable owners; avoid silently excluding hard-to-map accounts.

Reviewer conflicts

Flag self-review and high-risk access for additional scrutiny or documented approval according to policy. Never auto-approve ambiguous permissions.

Safe remediation

Keep collection read-only by default. Separate review decisions from write-capable removal, require authorization and verify the post-change state.

Reliable orchestration

Design for retries, idempotent campaign operations, partial failures, versioned snapshots and an append-only decision history.

06 / SUCCESS CRITERIA

Measure the system, not just campaign completion.

Coverage

% of in-scope apps and entitlements successfully inventoried

Reviewer effort

Median decision time and items per owner

Remediation SLA

Time from removal decision to verified revocation

Evidence quality

% of decisions linked to complete snapshots and audit trails

These are proposed measurement dimensions, not claimed production results. This engineering work is ongoing.
