CASE STUDY / SECURITY PLATFORM ENGINEERING

Making enterprise risk visible and actionable.

How I helped turn scattered security and risk processes into a shared platform for better decisions and accountability.

Organization: UberFocus: Risk management, issues, exceptions and controlsStatus: Delivered

01 / THE PROBLEM

Important decisions were spread across different processes.

When risks, security issues, exceptions and control information live in separate workflows, it becomes harder to understand priorities, identify owners and follow progress. Leaders need a consistent picture; teams need a practical way to act.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Bring risk workflows together.

I led work on Risk Central, a shared approach to managing enterprise risk information and related workflows. The goal was not simply to create another dashboard, but to make ownership, escalation and follow-through easier to manage.

  • Connect risk, issue, exception and control workflows in a common experience
  • Make owners, status and decisions easier to see and track
  • Support clearer reporting for teams and leadership
  • Reduce the friction of moving from identifying a problem to addressing it

03 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Build for decisions, not just records.

One shared view

Bring related security information together so people can understand context.

Clear ownership

Make it apparent who needs to decide or act next.

Actionable reporting

Show what needs attention rather than simply collecting more data.

04 / IMPACT

A more consistent way to manage enterprise risk.

The platform work improved visibility into risks and related actions and supported more consistent ownership and reporting. Related security issue management improvements contributed to a reported 70% reduction in the issue backlog and up to 3× faster remediation. Those figures describe the broader program, not a standalone measured effect of Risk Central.

This case study describes publicly shareable approaches and high-level outcomes. Internal architecture, implementation details and sensitive company information are intentionally omitted.
